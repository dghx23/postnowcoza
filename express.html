How PostNow Express Works

Dispatch a courier across South Africa in four effortless steps — all inside WhatsApp.

1
Chat & Share Location
Open a WhatsApp chat with PostNow Express. Drop a location pin or type pickup and drop-off addresses — no forms, no sign-ups.
2
Get Live Rate Options
Receive instant quotes across same-day, overnight, and standard delivery tiers aggregated from top SA couriers.
3
Pay via PayShap
Approve an instant PayShap Request-to-Pay prompt directly on your phone, or pay via a PayFast checkout link.
4
Track in WhatsApp
Get automatic WhatsApp updates when your driver is assigned, package collected, in-transit, and delivered.
