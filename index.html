ABOUT THE GROUP

About PostNow Group

PostNow Group is building the next generation of Southern African logistics, compliance, and commerce platforms. What began with regulated physical document dispatch (E2) has expanded into instant WhatsApp courier booking (Express), cross-border landed cost calculation (GlobeMe), and protected peer-to-peer escrow trade (Midl).

Our mission is to eliminate operational friction and fraud across Southern African commerce by combining compliance-first design, modern logistics APIs, and transparent financial instruments.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PostNow Group?
PostNow Group is a Southern African logistics and commerce company operating four specialized platforms: PostNow E2 (regulated documents), PostNow Express (WhatsApp courier), GlobeMe (US-to-SA shopping), and Midl (peer-to-peer escrow).
How do the platforms work together?
All four platforms share the same core backend infrastructure: Bob Go courier routing, PayFast/PayShap payment gateways, audit trail logging, and POPIA data protection controls.
What is PostNow E2 used for?
E2 is designed for banks, law firms, and compliance-heavy organizations that require physical paper printing, courier dispatch, wet-ink signature collection, and certified return pathways with immutable audit trails.
How does PostNow Express courier work?
Chat with PostNow over WhatsApp, select pickup and drop-off addresses, get an instant courier quote, pay via PayShap Request-to-Pay, and track delivery updates in chat.
How does GlobeMe calculate landed costs?
GlobeMe parses US product links or descriptions and calculates SACU import tariffs, local VAT (SA 15%, Botswana 14%, Namibia 15%), and international courier fees before purchase, comparing costs against local SA retailers.
What is Midl peer-to-peer escrow?
Midl protects buyers and sellers in peer-to-peer trades. Funds are held in escrow, verified couriers collect and inspect the item, and payment is released only upon verified delivery.
