About PostNow

PostNow is building the Southern African logistics and commerce platform for compliance-first operations. We started with regulated documents (E2), and are expanding to instant courier logistics (Express) and cross-border commerce (GlobeMe) — all under the same principles: transparency, POPIA-first design, and zero operational friction.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between E2, Express, and GlobeMe?
E2 is for regulated documents with chain-of-custody audit trails (law firms, banks). Express is for ad-hoc instant courier booking via WhatsApp with PayShap payment. GlobeMe is for browsing US products with transparent landed-cost calculation (duty + VAT) delivered to your SA address.
Can I use multiple PostNow products in one conversation?
Yes. All three products integrate seamlessly — you can book a courier via Express, track documents via E2, and browse GlobeMe products all within the same WhatsApp chat.
How does PostNow Express work?
Chat with PostNow over WhatsApp, select your pickup location and delivery address, get an instant quote, pay via PayShap Request-to-Pay, and receive tracking updates. No app download. No friction.
Does GlobeMe include duty and VAT in the price?
Yes. GlobeMe shows an itemized breakdown: product cost, shipping, import duty, and VAT — all calculated before checkout. No hidden fees or surprise customs charges.
Is PostNow POPIA compliant?
Yes. The entire platform — E2, Express, and GlobeMe — was designed with POPIA as a core requirement. We maintain full audit trails, access controls, and support data subject rights across all products.
How long does delivery take?
E2 documents: 1–2 business days (urban), 2–4 days (remote). Express courier: depends on service tier (same-day to 2-day available). GlobeMe shopping: 5–10 business days (US warehouse to SA address, including customs clearance).
Can I track my delivery?
Yes. All three products offer real-time tracking. E2 documents have immutable chain-of-custody logs, Express courier shipments update via WhatsApp, and GlobeMe orders show progress from US warehouse to your SA address.
Do you support high-volume or bulk orders?
Yes. E2 supports enterprise workflows, Express can handle recurring courier bookings, and GlobeMe scales for businesses doing multiple US imports. Contact us for volume pricing.
