COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PostNow Group?

PostNow Group is a Southern African logistics and commerce company operating four specialized platforms: PostNow E2 (regulated document dispatch), PostNow Express (WhatsApp courier), GlobeMe (US-to-SA import duty calculator), and Midl (peer-to-peer escrow trade).

How do the platforms share infrastructure?

All four platforms share the same core backend: Bob Go courier routing, PayFast/PayShap payment gateways, hash-chained audit trail logging, and POPIA data protection controls.

What is PostNow E2 used for?

E2 is designed for banks, law firms, and compliance-heavy organizations requiring physical document printing, courier dispatch, wet-ink signature collection, and certified return pathways with immutable audit logs.

How does PostNow Express work?

Chat with PostNow over WhatsApp, share pickup and drop-off addresses, get an instant courier quote, pay via PayShap Request-to-Pay, and receive live delivery updates directly in chat — zero app download needed.

How does GlobeMe calculate landed costs?

GlobeMe parses US product links or descriptions and calculates SACU import tariffs, local VAT (SA 15%, Botswana 14%, Namibia 15%), and international courier fees — before purchase, with local SA retailer price comparison.

What is Midl peer-to-peer escrow?

Midl protects buyers and sellers in peer-to-peer trades. Funds are held in escrow, a verified courier collects and inspects the item, and payment is only released upon verified delivery and condition confirmation.
