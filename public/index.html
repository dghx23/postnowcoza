How Midl works

We handle the money, the logistics, and the verification. You just handle the shopping.

STEP 1

Secure Payment

Buyer pays Midl. We lock the funds safely in our FSCA-regulated trust account.

STEP 2

Courier Dispatch

We schedule a tracked courier to collect from the seller. GPS visibility end-to-end.

STEP 3

Condition Report

On Verified Delivery and Premium Vault, the item comes to us first — both buyer and seller get the same report before it goes further.

STEP 4

Release & Pay

The courier's tracking number confirms delivery. That's what releases payment to the seller — not a manual click.

Two routes, one guarantee

Essentials and Standard go straight to the buyer. Verified Delivery and Premium Vault stop at our inspection hub first. Either way, payment only moves once delivery is proven.

ESSENTIALS · STANDARD

Direct Delivery

Courier collects, delivers straight to the buyer, payment releases on proof of delivery.

  1. Buyer pays into the trust account — funds locked
  2. Courier dispatched, seller gets a pickup window
  3. Courier collects — takes a condition photo at pickup
  4. Live tracking from pickup to the buyer's door
  5. Delivery photo + signature captured (proof of delivery)
  6. Proof of delivery releases payment to the seller automatically
~24h JHB↔CPT overnight · 2–6h same-city
VERIFIED DELIVERY · PREMIUM VAULT

Hub Inspection

Courier collects, item stops at our inspection hub, buyer sees proof before it moves again.

  1. Buyer pays into the trust account — funds locked
  2. Courier collects from the seller, condition photo taken
  3. Item arrives at Midl's inspection hub
  4. Full inspection: condition report, photos, serial numbers
  5. Report sent to buyer and seller — buyer has 24h to respond
  6. Accepts: onward courier to buyer, proof of delivery releases payment
  7. Rejects: item returned to seller, buyer refunded in full — no fees
+4–8h added for inspection · return cover included
