Funds are locked in Midl's FSCA‑regulated trust account. Both parties see the confirmation.

Why Johannesburg?

One city that delivers everything Midl needs to succeed.

Johannesburg contributes 16% of South Africa's GDP. It's home to the headquarters of most JSE‑listed companies and a high concentration of wealth.

The FSCA is based in Sandton. Being physically close to the regulator makes compliance meetings, audits, and relationship‑building seamless.

Tech & Talent Hub

Sandton and Rosebank are hotspots for tech startups, with a deep pool of software engineers, product managers, and fintech specialists.
